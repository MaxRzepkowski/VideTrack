Shapes built from Audiobookshelf's own source, not captured live
(advplyr/audiobookshelf@5de038d): `User.toOldJSONForBrowser()` for
`GET /api/me` with `MediaProgress.getOldMediaProgress()` entries (times in
milliseconds since the epoch), and `LibraryItem.toOldJSON()` with
`Book.toOldJSON()` for `GET /api/items/{id}` (audio files and chapters left
empty). Titles, authors and the ISBN are the real books'; ids, progress and
dates are made up.
