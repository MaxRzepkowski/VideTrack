The two layouts of an iCheckMovies export. `checks.csv` is an export from
the movies tab (checked, favorites, watchlist…), semicolon-separated as the
account can set it; `top-films.csv` an export of a list, comma-separated,
with `rank`, `rankdifference` and `dislikedcount` in front. `checked` holds
the moment of the check or `no`; the other flags `yes`/`no`; `imdburl` the
title's IMDb page. The counts and dates are made up.
